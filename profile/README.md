We're a student-led community at the University of Waterloo exploring how intelligent systems can make a useful difference in the physical world. Through engineering projects, industry visits, and conversations in the field, we bring different disciplines together to learn, build, and ask better questions.

### Our first project

A compact, modular mobile robot for precision crop care. We're starting with growers' experience and a carefully defined task. The project is in discovery and concept development.

### Teams

| Team | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Mechanical Design** | Mobile platform, mechanisms, task tools: CAD, prototyping, fabrication |
| **Electrical & Hardware** | Power distribution, motor interfaces, embedded systems, sensor integration |
| **Perception & Vision** | Camera systems, datasets, computer vision, 3D sensing |
| **Localization & Control** | Mapping, state estimation, planning, motion control: ROS 2, SLAM, simulation |
